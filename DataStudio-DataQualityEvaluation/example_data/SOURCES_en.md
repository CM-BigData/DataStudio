# Example Data Sources

Text samples are derived from Project Gutenberg open texts. Images are derived from the Oxford-IIIT Pet open-licensed image dataset. Audio samples come from the Mini LibriSpeech open-licensed speech dataset. Duplicate, low-resolution, blurred, blank, and corrupted samples are local derived variants of those open datasets.

For complete source, license, and local processing details, see [origin.md](origin.md).
